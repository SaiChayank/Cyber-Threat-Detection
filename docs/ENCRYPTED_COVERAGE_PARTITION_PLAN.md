## Minimum coverage required before training

These are new engineering coverage prerequisites, frozen before further source
contents. They are not official SIH dataset counts, statistical power guarantees
or replacements for the unchanged promotion gates.

1. At least **three distinct TLS-capable C2 framework families**, represented in
   separate train, validation and reserved roles; more are needed if additional
   families are used in training. Canonical aliases/versions of one framework do
   not create independent families.
2. At least **three independent collection source/environment provenance groups**,
   one per role, each with verified malicious and benign session intent. Publisher
   names, IP subnets, server hostnames and separate files alone do not establish
   independence. Training TQH-only positives versus Tranco-only negatives would
   preserve source/class confounding and is prohibited.
3. At least **two separate capture runs per role**, on different documented UTC
   dates/deployments. Rotations, copied artifacts, reconnects or slices of one run
   do not count. Runs sharing a framework/environment remain clustered replicates,
   not population-independent statistical trials.
4. In development, at least **two positive JA3 configurations in training** and
   **one genuinely shared positive/benign JA3 counterexample**. This addresses the
   observed single-fingerprint shortcut. Use real labelled observations; do not
   fabricate a fingerprint, drop conflicting examples or inspect reserved JA3s to
   select a passing cohort. This does not select new model features or authorize a
   fingerprint lookup detector.
5. The four benign application categories below must have documented coverage in
   every role, including complete benign-only control captures. Counts and source
   identities remain missing until attested/qualified; 467 generic benign labels
   do not automatically meet these requirements.
6. Training/validation inputs must pass the unchanged native acquisition gate and
   provenance grouping checks. An independent reserved supplier must be identified,
   acquired/sealed and have trustworthy label/coverage metadata. Its raw value
   qualification occurs without tuner access, or once during final evaluation
   after choices are frozen; failure blocks promotion.
7. A future candidate's exact causal feature/preprocessing/missingness contract must
   be frozen and match runtime. This data-plan task does not freeze a new feature
   order, modify the current extractor or validate a future artifact.

No sample/packet count is treated as a quality result. Report actual denominators,
framework/control breakdowns and uncertainty later; small gate-passing samples
cannot establish population false-positive rates.

## Required benign application controls

Require each category in **training, validation and reserve**, using different
concrete application/build/TLS-stack/deployment groups across roles:

- **Browser TLS:** verified interactive browsing/navigation, background requests
  and ordinary CDN/web resources; different browser/client builds where available.
  Destination popularity alone is not a browser-intent label.
- **Software updates:** genuine updater/package-download TLS, including larger
  transfers and periodic checking. A CDN or Ubuntu mirror destination alone is
  not verification that an updater generated the session.
- **API/SDK clients:** documented normal API requests, scheduled polling and
  telemetry, including small stable/periodic traffic that could resemble C2.
- **Ordinary encrypted applications:** documented legitimate sync, collaboration,
  messaging or other normal application sessions, including variable and sustained
  transfers. No generic port-443 or external-IP benign assumption.

Keep benign and malicious traffic collected in comparable environments, clocks,
capture formats and observation conditions. Record exact application and version,
intent provenance and session UID/tuple/time. Missing concrete application truth
blocks category credit. Broad categories recur across roles; exact build/deployment
groups do not. Shared TLS libraries/engine families are disclosed rather than
mistaken for application independence.

Current TQH background requests establish narrow controlled benign intent only.
Neither their two JA3s nor a source README's CDN/service list proves the four
application categories. Their role/category credit is therefore **unverified**.

## Leakage-safe grouping and causal boundary

Before extracting candidate features, build a canonical identity registry from
metadata. Connect any observations sharing collection provenance/related exports,
framework or known sample/campaign, agent deployment, collection run/rotations,
raw artifact hash, namespaced session identity or concrete benign build/deployment.
Overlapping intervals connect only when they share the deployment/run identity;
global timestamp, IP, port or JA3 equality alone is not proof of duplicate identity.
Keep each transitive component indivisible in its explicitly bound role. Missing
identity or conflicting roles is a blocker, not permission to split the component.

Verify zero cross-role overlap for framework, source/environment, runs/rotations,
samples/deployments, session identity, duplicate artifacts and benign app/build
groups once all actual roles exist. Preserve the historical quarantine separately;
no recycled historical-test row becomes a fresh final holdout. Exact duplicates
receive one evaluation identity; retransmissions retain their original events.
Do not rebalance by removing benign background or select records by predictions.

Use only passively observable prefix features with the same semantics during
training and inference. ClientHello fields become available at their observed
packet index/time, not connection start. Completed-session totals/duration or TLS
record lengths are not early IP-packet sizes. Missing reverse/handshake data stays
unavailable. Preserve raw order and document clipping, late events, duplicates and
parser-blind sessions; apply the existing late-event policy during future replay.
No new reassembly, raw QUIC extraction, return path, probing or decryption.

Source/environment, family/label, IP/endpoint role, capture/UID/filename, interval,
jitter, generator configuration and service/history annotations are grouping or
provenance metadata, never predictors. No new model/fingerprint feature is chosen
here. Any later fingerprint-derived feature needs matched legitimate controls and
novel positive configurations, plus exact causal runtime parity.

## Scenario-level gates and sealed reserve

Keep the [existing protocol](ENCRYPTED_SESSION_VALIDATION.md) exactly:
**recall >=80%, precision >=95%, benign-scenario FPR <=2%, with a qualifying
positive alert before the scenario's final event**. Confidence and severity remain
separate; alert IDs/flow/timestamps/evidence must match actual observations.

The primary unit is a complete, independently reset, causally replayed labelled
scenario, clustered by source/framework/run. A malicious full-run scenario is TP
only when an early alert maps to an explicitly malicious TLS session's UID/tuple/
time; an alert on its benign background cannot count as TP. A separately captured,
documented benign-only scenario is FP if it produces any encrypted-indicator alert.
Do not create it by deleting malicious/background packets from a mixed capture.

Session TP/TN/FP/FN, fingerprint and application breakdowns are secondary diagnostics.
Same-run sessions do not inflate independent scenario denominators. Preserve mixed
benign background and report its session false positives. Valid labelled TLS cases
missed by parsing/detection are not silently dropped from the declared input scope;
report visibility failures and missed detections. Unknown-intent observations do
not acquire forced labels, and inadequately labelled scenarios cannot establish
the quantitative gate.

Reserve access before training is limited to source/licence/identity/hash and
coverage attestation metadata, with packet, label-value and fingerprint contents
sealed from tuning. Before the one final read, freeze the candidate artifact/hash,
feature contract, role manifests, preprocessing/missingness, threshold, exact
scenario mapping, uncertainty calculation and unchanged gates. Full qualification
must either be attested without tuner value access or occur in that one-shot final
read. No qualifying reserve currently exists; this plan does not create a custodian
service or claim enforced isolation on the local filesystem.

If a final source or model fails, retain its report and keep the candidate disabled.
No gate lowering, source substitution, evaluation-unit change, session filtering or
reserved tuning. Failure analysis may recommend one unimplemented next experiment;
a new untouched dataset/protocol is needed for a subsequent candidate decision.
